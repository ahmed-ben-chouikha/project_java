## 💻 Example Interactions

### Conversation 1: Making a Tough Decision
```
👨‍💼 You: What about match fixing?

🤖 Bot: 🎯 PUNISHMENT RECOMMENDATION
        Violation: Match Fixing / Collusion
        Recommended: Game Ban (Permanent)
        Duration: Permanent
        [Details about integrity violation...]

👨‍💼 You: Is this always permanent?

🤖 Bot: 📊 SEVERITY ASSESSMENT
        This is a CRITICAL violation
        Involves competitive integrity
        [Context and guidance...]

👨‍💼 You: What are alternatives?

🤖 Bot: 🔄 ALTERNATIVE ACTIONS
        Primary: Game Ban (Permanent)
        Alternatives:
        • Longer Tournament Bans
        • Longer Match Bans
        • Combined with fines
        
        Choose based on:
        • Evidence strength
        • League policy
        • Player history
```

### Conversation 2: Progressive Discipline
```
👨‍💼 You: Tell me about cussing

🤖 Bot: [Shows 1-7 day first offense, 7-30 day repeat...]

👨‍💼 You: Guidelines?

🤖 Bot: 📋 PUNISHMENT GUIDELINES
        1️⃣ FIRST OFFENSE: 1-7 days (lower end)
        2️⃣ REPEAT: Escalate to 7-30 days
        3️⃣ SERIOUS: Use upper range
        4️⃣ CONTEXT: Check player history

👨‍💼 You: This is their 2nd offense

🤖 Bot: Based on the guidelines:
        Recommended: 7-14 day ban
        [Explanation of escalation...]
```
